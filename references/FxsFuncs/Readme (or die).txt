 = ATTENTION:
This mod is a mod for mods. If you install it in your game the way it comes here in the download it will do:
Increased the limit of special effects in gameplay (can fix crash with very complex effects mods),
Fixed an original game memory leak.
Effects are now colored by distant fog.
Fixed wrong rain effect at high or low FPS.
Reactivates effects colliding with the surfaces (such as bullet shells from guns).

You may want to take a look at the .ini file, some things can be useful.

This mod's greatest utility is to be used in conjunction with other mods that change and add special effects to the game.

The mod was created mainly for effects mods created by Ezekiel, with his mods, specially the new ones, you can learn how he used FxsFuncs to make everything possible.

Do not use this mod with badly configured ini file (if you uninstall an effect mod that uses settings in this mod, prefer to remove this mod or restore default .ini to avoid errors).

It is compatible with EffectsLoader, and rarely causes any conflict with other mods, since practically (or) no mod in the world edits GTA SA's special effects system.

It is allowed to include this mod in the compilation of other mods that use it, as long as you keep clear credits and try to keep updated (prefer to put a link to original mod).
