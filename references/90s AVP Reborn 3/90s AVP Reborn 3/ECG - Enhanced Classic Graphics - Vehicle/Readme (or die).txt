------- Instructions:

Choose the folder you prefer (HQ or HD) and take the folder inside it (ECG - VehicleTXD) and put it in your ModLoader folder.

The "ImVehFt" folder is optional for replacing IVF dirt and license plates with this mod.
Simply replace your ImVehFt folder with this one to replace the files with the new ones.

-- Download the latest version of Modloader: MixMods.com.br/2015/01/SA-Modloader.html



 "HD" = High-definition = High definition, for those who want textures in the best definition. [Average of 3X more defined than the originals; a little above necessary]
 "HQ" = High-quality = High quality, for those who want the textures in good quality and not lose FPS. [Average 2X more defined than originals; enough for good definition]

 Note that even in HD, the processing weight is still little, as these textures are shared equally for all vehicles in the game.


 Copyright: Do NOT use these textures within any other .txd or product, be it another vehicle.txd, any .txd of any car, or other game.
            If you want to use these textures on your car, create it using these textures directly from vehicle.txd, and in the publication send the official download of this vehicle.txd. Except the textures of headlights.
            The textures of the headlights are edited from HQMLv2, authors: Pumbars (spc.gta.com.ua), Slayer (gta-customs.com.ru), Spit811, DenX, Torzer and Andrey P.
