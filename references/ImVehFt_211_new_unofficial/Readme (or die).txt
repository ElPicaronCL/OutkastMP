=======   MixMods.com.br   ======= - Best visualization with monospaced font, as "Consolas".


------- Instructions:

Extract the folder "ImVehFt" to some folder inside your Modloader folder. Or if you prefer to your GTA SA directory.

-- Download the latest version of Modloader: MixMods.com.br/2015/01/SA-Modloader.html


 - If you are a modeler and want to adapt cars for this mod, take a look at "(doc - for modelers)" folder.
 - ImVehFtFix.asi is already included: MixMods.com.br/2019/09/ImVehFtFix.html

 = SAMP: If problems in SAMP, open ImVehFt.ini and set "SAMP_fix=1".


Commands: (customizable in ImVehFt.ini)
J     - On/Off fog lights
Z     - On/Off left turn signal
C     - On/Off right turn signal
X     - On/Off warning (all turn signals)
Shift - turn off turn signals
