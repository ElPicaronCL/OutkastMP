=======   MixMods.com.br   ======= - Best visualization with monospaced fonts, like "Consolas".


------- Instructions:

Extract the "shaders" folder to your GTA SA folder with RenderHook 2020 (rh2020_1_2_a) installed.
It will replace the original RenderHook files.

- Download of RenderHook: MixMods.com.br/2018/03/renderhook-by-petkagta.html


 == Settings:
Replaces RenderHook cloud rendering with CloudWorks rendering.
Enable clouds in the RenderHook in-game menu, or EnableCloudRendering="true" in the settings.xml file.


CloudWorks version: v1.0 (RH)
